The fine print, in plain words

First-time clients sign this once at the counter. After that we only ask again if something changes.

Vaccinations

Rabies is required for every dog and cat, with proof from your vet. We also recommend bordetella for dogs, since they share the air with other dogs all day. Your vet can email records to us before your first visit.

Fleas

If we find fleas, we switch to a flea bath ($25) before anything else and clean the room behind them. We text you so you can treat the house and the bedding too.

Matting

We comb out for up to 30 minutes at $15 per 15. Past that, we call and recommend a shave-down. Comfort comes before length, every time.

Nervous or snappy pets

Tell us when you book. If a pet might bite, we use a soft basket muzzle: it keeps everyone safe and lets us keep going gently. We never sedate. If a pet is too stressed to finish, we stop, call you and charge only for the time used.

Seniors and special needs

Ruth takes most of our seniors. Older dogs get a mat to stand on, breaks and extra time at no charge. For heart conditions or seizures, we may ask for a note from your vet first.

Running late

There’s a 15-minute grace period. After that we may need to shorten the service or move you to another day.

Cancelling

Please give us 24 hours. Cancellations inside 24 hours and no-shows are $30. Sick pets are the exception: just call.

Pick-up

We text a photo and a pick-up time. Please collect within an hour of that text. Dogs who wait rest on a bed behind the counter, never in a crate.

Dee Marsh

Asked at the counter

The questions we answer most, on the phone and over the counter. Anything else, call 07 00 21 42 82.

How often should my dog be groomed?

It depends on the coat. Curly and doodle coats: every 4 to 6 weeks. Long silky coats like shih tzus and yorkies: every 4 to 8. Double coats: a deshed each spring and fall, with a bath in between. Short coats: a bath and nails every 6 to 8 weeks, or whenever they roll in something.

– Nell

Can I stay and watch?

You’re welcome to stay for the hand-over, and for a puppy’s first visit. Most dogs settle faster once their person has gone, so we don’t have owners in the grooming room. We text a photo the minute they’re done.

– Tomás

Do you sedate?

No, never. We aren’t vets, and sedation on a grooming table isn’t safe. If a pet can’t cope, we stop, call you, and plan a few shorter visits instead.

– Nell

What shampoo do you use?

An oat and aloe wash on most dogs (it’s on the shelf by the till), a fragrance-free one for sensitive skin, and a whitening shampoo on request. If your vet has prescribed a shampoo, bring it in and we’ll use that.

– Ruth

Why do doodles cost more?

A doodle coat tangles faster, holds more water and takes longer to scissor evenly. The extra $20 covers about 30 more minutes on the table. Brushing down to the skin between visits keeps dematting off the bill.

– Nell

Any tips for a first visit?

Walk them before drop-off, bring rabies proof, and tell us what you liked or hated about their last groom. A photo of a cut you love helps more than any description. A first full groom runs a little long while we get to know them.

– Ruth

Do you take walk-ins?

For nails, yes: Tuesday to Saturday, 1:00 to 4:00. The self-wash tubs are always walk-in. Full grooms, bath and tidies, desheds and the cat room need a booking.

– Tomás
