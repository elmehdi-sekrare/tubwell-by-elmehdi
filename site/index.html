A full groom, start to finish

  1. 8:30

    Hand-over

    Five minutes at the counter. We check coat, skin, ears and nails, and write your notes on their card.

  2. 8:45

    Bath

    Two washes with a shampoo picked for the coat, then conditioner and an ear clean.

  3. 9:30

    Hand dry

    Towel, then a quiet high-velocity dryer held by a person. Never a heated cage dryer.

  4. 10:15

    Cut & finish

    Scissor and clipper work on one table, one pet at a time. Nails ground smooth.

  5. 11:40

    We text you

    A photo and a pick-up time. Anything we noticed, like a lump or a sore ear, goes in the message too.

Our house rules

  1. Hand-dried, never caged

    Heat in a closed box is how dogs get hurt at groomers. Every pet here is dried by a person holding the dryer.

  2. No sedation, ever

    If a pet is too stressed to groom, we stop, call you and book shorter visits instead.

  3. Double coats stay on

    Huskies, goldens and shepherds get a deshed. We only clip them when a vet asks us to.

  4. Never alone on the table

    Nobody stands on a grooming table without a groomer’s hands on them. Not for a second.

  5. Seniors set the pace

    Older dogs get a mat to stand on, breaks between steps and extra time at no charge.
